2018 and 2019 ENERGY STAR® Partner of the Year
Marathon Petroleum received the Environmental Protection Agency's (EPA) annual award in 2018 and 2019 for our progress to increase energy efficiency across all operations. Since the EPA began that program in 2006, we've been recognized more than all other U.S. refiners combined. Marathon Petroleum is the only petroleum refining company to earn the award in 2018 and 2019.
