our employees put values into action
We’re a global team united by a shared set of values, called The Chevron Way. From volunteering initiatives to a commitment to diversity and inclusion, you’ll see that our values are so much more than words on a screen – they’re our way of life.

volunteering
around the world
Left:
Employees in the Luanda Chevron Volunteer Opportunities Program helped 100 children reduce exposure to malaria by painting the Mama Muxima Orphanage walls with insect-repellent paint.
Right:
As part of the One Goal – One Team campaign in Thailand, more than 550 Chevron employees and family members joined community members to prepare land to become the first “Pa Sak Model” Learning Center, which promotes natural resource conservation, in the Huai Krathaek region.
chevron
humankind
Through Chevron Humankind, employees, retirees and contractors in the United States provide support for their communities throughout the year.
Top:
Twice a year, Chevron Richmond employees conduct mock interviews with Contra Costa County ROP students. In 2016, 73 employees and 130 ROP students participated in the mock interviews, and employees contributed 803 hours to the program.
Left:
Since 1997, more than 3,400 Chevron Houston employees have volunteered 95,000 hours with Habitat Humanity. Through the partnership, Chevron has contributed $2.83 million in home sponsorships and Chevron Humankind employee donations.
Right:
Since Chevron's Law Function Pro Bono program launched in 2014, more than 90 lawyers and nonlawyer professionals have partnered with various organizations in Texas, Louisiana and California to provide more than 1,000 hours of legal services for veterans, immigrants, seniors, at-risk youth and others in need of legal assistance.
