volunteering
around the world
In countries around the world, Chevron employees are applying their skills, experience and energy in volunteer activities that help improve education, provide basic needs, foster new business opportunities and ultimately strengthen the communities in which we operate.
Chevron has a long history of support for cleft lip and palate surgeries in Venezuela in partnership with Operation Smile Foundation and other institutions. Since 1998, more than 3,000 patients have been seen, and our employee volunteers have provided valuable support by helping patients with medical forms and assisting with interpretations.
Chevron volunteers in Aberdeen, Scotland participate in the Engineers 'n’ Our Lives initiative, which enables students to demonstrate STEM skills in real-life settings. More than 10,000 schoolchildren have taken part in the program, giving them the opportunity to appreciate the role engineering plays in their lives.
Chevron Thailand partnered with the Institute of Sufficiency Economy and the Agri-Nature Foundation to initiate “The Power of Human Energy: A Journey Inspired by the King” project in 2013. Since then, over 2,000 Chevron Thailand volunteers have helped build earthen dikes, dig irrigation ponds, build check dams and plant trees.
chevron
humankind
Through Chevron Humankind, employees, retirees and contractors in the United States provide support for their communities throughout the year.
For World AIDS Day 2017, 145 employees in San Ramon assembled 5,700 personal hygiene kits that are being distributed to clients of the San Francisco AIDS Foundation and Cal-PEP.
Each month, Salt Lake Refinery employees volunteer at a mobile food pantry that provides food to nearly 100 families at a neighboring elementary school.
In cooperation with the Freeport-McMoRan Audubon Species Survival Center, Chevron volunteers in Louisiana are helping to breed, hatch, and rear whooping crane chicks to supplement the wild population. In 2017, Chevron volunteers spent 146 hours to help keep the habitat sustainable for breeding programs.
