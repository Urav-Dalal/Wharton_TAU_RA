Mission, Vision & Values of Bristol-Myers Squibb Company
To our patients and customers
We commit to scientific excellence and investment in biopharmaceutical research and development to provide innovative, high-quality medicines that address the unmet medical needs of patients with serious diseases. We apply scientific rigor to produce clinical and economic benefit through medicines that improve patients’ lives. We strive to make information about our commercialized medicines widely and readily available.
To our employees
We embrace a diverse workforce and inclusive culture. The health, safety, professional development, work-life balance and equitable, respectful treatment of our employees are among our highest priorities.
To our shareholders
We strive to produce sustained strong performance and shareholder value.
To our environment
We encourage the preservation of natural resources and strive to minimize the environmental impact of our operations and products.
